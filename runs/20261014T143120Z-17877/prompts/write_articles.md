# Article drafting task

Write one 600–900 word Markdown article per brief for the configured audience. Preserve every claim ID in an HTML comment of the form `<!-- claim:01-c1 -->`. Distinguish facts, attributed source claims, and interpretation in ordinary reader-facing prose. Include an as-of date, one meaningful limitation or counterargument, practical implications, AI-assistance disclosure, and linked source notes with timestamps for audio/video. Do not add factual claims not present in the brief.
