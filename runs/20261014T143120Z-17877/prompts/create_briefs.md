# Three-brief synthesis task

Using accepted decisions and retained evidence, return exactly three JSON brief records with piece IDs `01`, `02`, and `03`.

- Piece 01 asks what changed and matters.
- Piece 02 tests which claims withstand scrutiny and where evidence is in tension.
- Piece 03 explains implications and what to watch next.

Each brief needs a distinct argument, synthesis across at least three independent publishers, a meaningful limitation or counterargument, and claims whose types are `reported_fact`, `source_claim`, or `inference`. Every claim must include exact versioned evidence locators. A text locator quotes an exact passage; a media locator names an existing transcript segment. Explain the reasoning added by the synthesis. Do not fill a coverage gap with unsupported prose; fail clearly if the evidence is insufficient.
