## Counterargument and limit

The evidence window is short, vendor reporting is selective, and the source set does not establish long-run causal effects. A plausible counterargument is that rapid product iteration makes a short evidence window more representative than older evaluations. That is partly right, but freshness cannot substitute for controls, comparison groups, or post-deployment observation. The responsible conclusion is therefore directional and revisable.
