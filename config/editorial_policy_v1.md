# Agent Operations Brief — Editorial Policy (revision 1, preserved for comparison)

Select recent sources relevant to small-team adoption of AI coding agents. Prefer credible, novel evidence and a mix of research, product announcements, and practitioner experience. Distinguish facts, claims, and interpretation; note uncertainty, commercial incentives, and unsupported assertions. Reject irrelevant or undated material.

This initial policy was too general: it did not cap publisher concentration or define which operational measures were needed before a capability claim could support adoption advice. Revision 2 in `editorial_policy.md` addresses those observed weaknesses.
