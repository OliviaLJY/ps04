Can an AI agent look at a 2D image
that uses complex architectural geometry
 and build it inside Blender on its own?
This is how I use Gemini 4 Argon
and Antigravity to do so.
I started in Flow, prompting for an empty catherdral
with specific lighting and angles.
I chose this particular image
of the repeating arches and columns
that creates this perspective effect.
Next, I fed that image into my terminal
connecting Antigravity directly to Blender via the MCP.
I kicked off an agentic loop prompt.
For 45 rounds, the model iteratively built the base mesh.
An agent even automatically overlaid
these comparison strips
against the reference image
to get the geometry closer,
iteration after iteration.
Once the structure was locked,
I paused the CLI to trigger target targeted micro-loops
for column fluting and floor textures.
Then came lighting.
The model automatically dialed in volumetric fog,
camera depth and audio-reactive sunlight
And here's the final result:
from a flat 2D prompt
to a fully realized
light-mapped, 3D cathedral walkthrough.
All driven by Gemini 4 Argon in Antigravity.
